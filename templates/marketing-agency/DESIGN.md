## Overview

This is the design system for a marketing agency website — a site whose job is to convince a marketing lead at a startup or SME that this agency has opinions, has done the work, and is worth a strategy call. It is built on a contradiction the brand wants: the warmth of paper and the bluntness of a poster. The chrome runs on a warm ivory canvas (`{colors.canvas}` — `#FFFDF7`) — not white — with a deeper paper tone (`{colors.surface-soft}` — `#F6F2E6`) for alternating bands, and every card, button and input is drawn with a chunky 2px ink border (`{colors.hairline}` — `#1A1523`) and a hard offset shadow, as if cut from card stock and laid on the desk. Type sits in Syne for headlines — heavy, slightly eccentric, tracked tight — and Instrument Sans for body; Space Mono labels everything that is a tag, an eyebrow or a caption under a number. Electric violet (`{colors.primary}` — `#6D28D9`) is the only button colour; acid yellow (`{colors.accent}` — `#FDE047`) is a highlighter pen, dragged under links, behind a key phrase in a headline, and across the tag on a case-study card.

The system has a distinctive **two-mode body layout**: marketing pages (home, services, process, team) stack bordered cards on the ivory canvas and alternate into `{colors.surface-soft}` bands, while case-study pages add a sticky 240px left sidebar (`{component.case-study-sidebar}`) listing the client's sector, services used, timeline and the results summary. Results numerals are the system's most distinctive visual moment: an 80px Syne figure in `{component.results-tile}` with a Space Mono caption beneath — and every one of them is a placeholder until a real, verifiable client result replaces it.

Sections stack at `{spacing.section}` (96px) rhythm. The ivory canvas is interrupted twice per page at most: once by the ink-dark `{component.manifesto-band}` (the agency's point of view in 40px Syne on `{colors.surface-dark}`), and once by the footer, which uses the same dark surface. There are no gradients, no mesh backgrounds, no illustration mascots; the visual identity is border weight, offset shadow, highlighter yellow and a few very large numbers.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm ivory canvas (`{colors.canvas}` — #FFFDF7) with a second paper tone (`{colors.surface-soft}` — #F6F2E6) for alternating bands — the page is paper, not screen
- Single electric-violet CTA (`{colors.primary}` — #6D28D9) with ivory text (`{colors.on-primary}`) — the only button colour in the system
- Acid-yellow highlighter (`{colors.accent}` — #FDE047) for link underlines, headline marks and tags — never a button fill
- Chunky 2px ink borders (`{colors.hairline}`) and hard offset shadows (`{shadows.offset-md}` — `5px 5px 0 0`) on every card; no blur, no soft elevation
- Syne 700/800 display with negative tracking, Instrument Sans 400/500 body, Space Mono for eyebrows, tags and stat captions
- 4px radius vocabulary across every component; only pill chips are fully rounded
- Pastel callouts (`{colors.accent-blue-soft}`, `{colors.accent-green-soft}`, `{colors.accent-red-soft}`, `{colors.accent-soft}`) break up long-form case-study body for context, outcome, caveat and highlight notes
- Case-study pages add a sticky 240px `{component.case-study-sidebar}` with client facts and a "Book a strategy call" CTA at the bottom

## Typography

### Font Family
**Syne** is the display face — a geometric grotesque with wide, slightly odd proportions that turn heavy at 700 and 800. Every headline, section title, card title and stat numeral is Syne. Weights are limited to 700 and 800: 800 for the hero, section headlines and stat numerals; 700 for card and sub-section headings. Syne ships 400–800, so no requested weight is missing. Tracking is negative at display sizes (-1.5px at 64px, -2px on the 80px numeral) so the wide letterforms lock together into a poster block.

**Instrument Sans** carries body: paragraphs, service descriptions, case-study prose, nav links, form labels and button labels. Weights 400 and 500 only — 500 for emphasis, nav and buttons, never bolder; Syne owns bold.

**Space Mono** is the labelling voice. Section eyebrows (`{typography.heading-sm}`), tags (`{typography.caption-xs}`), footer category headers (`{typography.utility-xs}`), captions beneath results numerals (`{typography.caption-md}`) and client-fact labels in the sidebar (`{typography.mono-xs}`) are all Space Mono, mostly uppercase with +1px tracking. Mono is taxonomy, never body: do not set a paragraph in it.

### Principles
Hierarchy is built from face contrast first, weight second. Syne at 800 does not need colour or size games to dominate; Instrument Sans at 400 does not compete; Space Mono at 11–14px uppercase marks taxonomy without shouting. Display sizes use tight negative tracking and a 1.0–1.1 line-height so headlines read as blocks; body settles at 17px / 1.5. Highlighter yellow behind a two- or three-word phrase in a `{typography.display-xl}` headline is the brand's signature typographic move — use it once per hero, never on every line.

### Note on Numerals
Results numerals are the most persuasive element on the site and the most dangerous. Set them in `{typography.stat-numeral}` with `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums` and a `{typography.caption-md}` Space Mono caption beneath ("pipeline growth, 6 months", "cost per lead, before → after"). Every figure must be a real, client-approved result. The template ships with obvious placeholders ("00%") so nothing fabricated can slip into production.

## Layout

### Spacing System
- **Base unit:** 8px (with finer 2/4/6px steps for tight inline gaps in tags and pill chips).
- **Tokens (front matter):** `{spacing.xxs}` (2px) · `{spacing.xs}` (4px) · `{spacing.sm}` (8px) · `{spacing.md}` (12px) · `{spacing.lg}` (16px) · `{spacing.xl}` (24px) · `{spacing.xxl}` (32px) · `{spacing.section}` (96px).
- **Universal section rhythm:** every page uses `{spacing.section}` (96px) as the vertical gap between major content blocks. Card grids use `{spacing.xl}` (24px) gutters — wider than usual so the offset shadows have room — and in-card padding sits at `{spacing.xl}` (24px) for service and case-study cards and `{spacing.xxl}` (32px) for process step cards.

### Grid & Container
- **Max width:** ~1280px content area at desktop with 24px gutters (~48px at ultrawide). Case-study article body sits at ~720px max width with the 240px sidebar pushing the article column right of centre.
- **Service grid:** 3-up at desktop, 2-up at 1024px, 1-up at 768px. Each `{component.service-card}` is a numbered card (01, 02, 03 in Space Mono) with a title, two-line description and a "See how →" link.
- **Case-study grid:** 2-up at desktop with a large 3:2 image, a `{component.badge-tag}` sector tag, title and one placeholder results line; 1-up at tablet.
- **Process:** 4 or 5 `{component.process-step-card}` items in a horizontal row at desktop (numbered 01–05 with a 2px ink connector line), stacked vertically at tablet.
- **Team grid:** 4-up at desktop, 2-up at tablet, 1-up at mobile; 1:1 portraits.
- **Case-study layout:** desktop 240px sticky left sidebar + ~720px article body = ~1000px, left-aligned within the 1280px container.
- **Footer:** 4-column link grid on `{colors.surface-dark}` at desktop, 2-up at tablet, 1-up at mobile.

### Whitespace Philosophy
Whitespace is generous on marketing pages and tight inside case studies. The home and services pages stack bordered cards with 24px gutters and 24px internal padding, with 96px between sections, while case-study articles tighten paragraph spacing to `{spacing.md}` (12px) to read like a report. The ivory canvas runs continuously through every section; the only breaks are the alternating `{colors.surface-soft}` band (at most every other section), the dark `{component.manifesto-band}` once per page, and 2px ink rules under section eyebrows.

## Elevation & Depth

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Flat | No border, no shadow | Canvas-on-canvas blocks, hero text, body sections, soft bands |
| 1 — Ink border | 2px solid `{colors.hairline}` | Every card, every input, every secondary button, table outer rule |
| 2 — Offset shadow | 2px border + `{shadows.offset-md}` (5px 5px 0 0 ink) | Service cards, case-study cards, team cards, process steps — the default card treatment |
| 3 — Offset shadow large / primary | 2px border + `{shadows.offset-lg}` (8px) or `{shadows.offset-primary}` (violet) | Featured case study, the contact form card, pressed state moves the card onto its shadow |
| 4 — Inverted dark band | `{colors.surface-dark}` fill | Manifesto band and footer — the system's only inverted surfaces |

The system has no soft (blurred) shadows anywhere. Depth is a hard offset: the card appears lifted off the paper by a solid ink block. On press, a card or button translates by the shadow offset and the shadow collapses to zero — the "stamped down" interaction that gives the system its tactility.

### Decorative Depth
Depth comes entirely from border weight, offset shadows and the highlighter, not from imagery effects:
- **Offset shadows** — solid, un-blurred, ink-coloured (or violet on featured cards). The primary depth device.
- **Highlighter marks** — `{colors.accent}` background blocks behind headline phrases, rendered with a slight `-1deg` skew via a pseudo-element so they look hand-dragged.
- **Pastel callouts** — `{component.callout-blue}` / `-green` / `-red` / `-highlight` tinted panels inside case-study articles, each with a Space Mono label (CONTEXT / OUTCOME / CAVEAT / OUR TAKE).
- **Manifesto band** — full-bleed `{colors.surface-dark}` panel carrying the agency's point of view in 40px Syne, with one phrase marked in `{colors.accent}`.
- **Photography** — case-study imagery and team portraits sit inside 2px-bordered frames with the same offset shadow as cards; images are treated as objects on the desk, not as backgrounds.

## Shapes

### Border Radius Scale

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `{rounded.none}` | 0px | Sub-nav strip, footer, sidebar, primary nav, manifesto band, tags — flat structural surfaces |
| `{rounded.xs}` | 2px | Mono label chips, highlighter pseudo-elements |
| `{rounded.sm}` | 4px | Inline buttons, form inputs, micro chips |
| `{rounded.md}` | 4px | Cards, callouts, every standard CTA (same value as sm — kept as a separate token) |
| `{rounded.lg}` | 4px | Large containers (same value — the system is deliberately flat) |
| `{rounded.full}` | 9999px | Pill filter chips only |

The radius vocabulary is 4px for nearly everything; rounded corners soften a hard-edged poster and this system wants to stay sharp. Only the compact filter pills are fully rounded, and `{component.badge-tag}` is square-cornered so it reads as a label-maker strip.

### Photography Geometry
Photography is real work and real people, never stock:
- **Case-study hero image** — 3:2 at desktop, 4:3 at mobile, inside a 2px ink frame with `{shadows.offset-lg}`.
- **Case-study card image** — 3:2 at `{rounded.md}` top edge, clipped by the card.
- **Team portraits** — 1:1, consistent crop and background across the whole team, 2px frame, `{shadows.offset-sm}`.
- **In-article imagery** — screenshots of campaigns, ad creatives and reports at native aspect, 2px frame, captioned in Space Mono.
- All images are placeholders until the agency supplies real, client-approved assets; never show a logo or campaign the agency did not make.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `{colors.canvas}` (ivory — `#FFFDF7`) as the page body. Never substitute pure white as the canvas.
- Reserve `{colors.primary}` (violet) for buttons, the featured shadow and focus rings. "Book a strategy call" is the brand's anchor.
- Use `{colors.accent}` (acid yellow) as a highlighter — underlines, headline marks, tags — never as a button fill.
- Draw every card, input and button with a 2px `{colors.hairline}` border and a hard offset shadow. Blurred shadows break the paper-and-ink illusion.
- Set headlines in Syne 700/800, body in Instrument Sans 400/500, and every eyebrow, tag and stat caption in Space Mono uppercase.
- Stack sections at `{spacing.section}` (96px) and alternate into `{colors.surface-soft}` at most every other section.
- Use `{component.callout-blue}` / `-green` / `-red` / `-highlight` only inside case-study articles, and use the red "Caveat" callout honestly.
- Ship `{component.results-tile}` with placeholder "00%" figures and replace them only with real, client-approved results.
- Put one `{component.manifesto-band}` per page where the agency's opinion matters most.

### Don't
- Don't use soft, blurred drop shadows. Elevation is a hard offset or nothing.
- Don't add a second saturated button colour. Violet is the only action colour; yellow is a pen.
- Don't replace the ivory canvas with pure white or stack several dark bands. One manifesto band plus the footer is the limit.
- Don't fabricate results, client names, logos or testimonials. A placeholder is better than an invented number.
- Don't use the callout pastels (`{colors.accent-blue-soft}`, `-green`, `-red`, `-soft`) as marketing-card backgrounds. They belong to case-study body only.
- Don't set body copy in Syne or Space Mono. Syne is for headlines; mono is for labels.
- Don't round corners above 4px except on `{component.pill-tab}`. The system is sharp by design.
- Don't pad cards with 32px+ on all sides except for `{component.process-step-card}`. Standard cards sit at 24px internal padding.

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| ultrawide | 1920px+ | Content max-width holds at 1280px; outer gutters grow to ~80px |
| desktop-large | 1440px | Default — 3-up service grid, 2-up case-study grid, 240px sticky sidebar visible |
| desktop | 1280px | Same layout with narrower outer gutters |
| desktop-small | 1024px | 3-up services collapse to 2-up; process row stays horizontal with tighter cards; sidebar remains visible |
| tablet | 768px | 2-up grids collapse to 1-up; process stacks vertically; sidebar collapses into a top facts card; primary nav becomes hamburger |
| mobile | 480px | Single-column everything; hero `{typography.display-xl}` scales 64px → ~40px; stat numerals 80px → 56px; offset shadows reduce to `{shadows.offset-sm}` |
| mobile-narrow | 320px | Section padding tightens to 48px; results tiles stack |

### Touch Targets
All interactive elements meet WCAG AA (≥ 44×44px). `{component.button-primary}` and `{component.button-secondary}` sit at 48px height with 20px padding. `{component.text-input}` and `{component.select-input}` sit at 48px. `{component.pill-tab}` is ~32–36px visual height with inline padding extending to ~44px tappable. Sidebar items use 14px text with ~32px line-height + 6px vertical padding for ~44px tap rows. Pressed-state translation (3px) must not move the tap target out from under a finger — animate the shadow, not the hit area.

### Collapsing Strategy
- **Primary nav:** desktop horizontal cluster → tablet hamburger drawer at 768px. The violet "Book a strategy call" CTA stays visible at every breakpoint (compact label on mobile).
- **Sub-nav strip:** desktop breadcrumb + next link → mobile next-link only.
- **Service grid:** 3-up → 2-up → 1-up at 1024 and 768px; gutters drop from 24px to 16px on mobile so shadows still clear.
- **Case-study grid:** 2-up → 1-up at tablet; the featured card keeps its violet shadow.
- **Process row:** horizontal 5-up with connector line → vertical stack at tablet with the connector running down the left edge.
- **Case-study layout:** desktop 240px sidebar + 720px article → tablet sidebar collapses to a facts card above the article → mobile facts card becomes a 2-column mono-label grid.
- **Results tiles:** 3-up → 3-up compact at tablet → stacked at mobile.
- **Footer:** 4-up link columns → 2-up at tablet → 1-up at mobile; the closing line and CTA stay at the top.
- **Section padding:** `{spacing.section}` (96px) desktop → 64px tablet → 48px mobile.
- **Hero headline:** `{typography.display-xl}` (64px) at desktop, scaling 52px / 44px / 40px down the stack; the highlighter mark reflows with the phrase.

### Image Behavior
Imagery is real work in bordered frames:
- **Case-study images** scale fluidly inside their 2px frames; fixed `aspect-ratio` boxes prevent layout shift. Serve AVIF/WebP with lazy loading below the fold.
- **Team portraits** stay 1:1 at every breakpoint.
- **Offset shadows** on image frames reduce from 8px to 5px to 3px down the breakpoint stack so they do not eat gutter space on mobile.
- **Highlighter marks** are CSS pseudo-elements and reflow with their text; they must never be baked into images.

## Iteration Guide

1. Focus on ONE component at a time. Pull its YAML entry and verify every property resolves.
2. Reference component names and tokens directly (`{colors.primary}`, `{component.button-primary-pressed}`, `{shadows.offset-md}`) — do not paraphrase.
3. Run `npx @google/design.md lint DESIGN.md` after edits — `broken-ref`, `contrast-ratio`, and `orphaned-tokens` warnings flag issues automatically.
4. Add new variants as separate component entries (`-pressed`, `-disabled`, `-focused`) — do not bury them inside prose.
5. Default body to `{typography.body-md}` (17px / 400 / 1.5); reach for `{typography.body-strong}` for emphasis; reserve `{typography.display-xl}` strictly for the hero and `{typography.stat-numeral}` for results tiles.
6. Keep `{colors.primary}` scarce per viewport — at most one violet button per fold.
7. Keep `{colors.accent}` scarcer than it feels — one highlighter mark per headline, one tag row per card. If the page looks like a revision notebook, halve the yellow.
8. When introducing a new component, ask whether it can be expressed with the existing 2px-border + offset-shadow + 4px-radius + ivory-canvas vocabulary before adding new tokens. The system's strength is that it almost never needs new ones.

## Known Gaps

- **Mobile screenshots not captured** — responsive behaviour is synthesised from the breakpoint stack; verify the sidebar-to-facts-card collapse and shadow reductions on real devices.
- **Hover states not documented** by system policy. Cards should lift by one shadow step on hover; links should fill with highlighter; specify both when building.
- **Results data** — every numeral in `{component.results-tile}` and every results line on `{component.case-study-card}` is a placeholder; the agency must supply verifiable figures and client permission before publishing.
- **Blog / insights** — undecided; if added, use `{component.case-study-card}` chrome with a `{component.badge-uppercase}` "INSIGHT" prefix and the same sidebar pattern.
- **Pricing approach** — the system supports a pricing-approach page (retainer / project / performance, explained in prose with `{component.service-card}` chrome) but no price figures are designed; those are placeholders.
- **Authenticated chrome** (client portal, reporting dashboards) is not in scope.
- **Form validation states** beyond the focused violet ring: use `{colors.accent-red}` for error text and border, `{colors.accent-green}` for success; not fully specified.

### Accessibility notes

- Violet on `surface-dark` is 1.9:1, so dark grounds carry `.nim-on-dark`, which swaps `--t-focus` to the acid yellow. Every focus ring on a dark band is yellow.
- The acid-yellow highlighter is decoration behind ink text; the phrase it marks is never the only way to read the sentence.
- The giant ghost word behind the testimonials and the wordmark at the foot of the footer are `aria-hidden` decoration. The real heading sits in the flow.
- Every counting figure renders its final value for assistive tech beside an `aria-hidden` ticking one, and renders the final value immediately under reduced motion.
