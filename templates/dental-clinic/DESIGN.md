## Overview

This template is the website of a dental clinic: treatments, the clinical team, online booking, new-patient information and forms, insurance and pricing, opening hours and location, emergency guidance, and an FAQ. The voice is clean, friendly, and reassuring. The homepage opens with a warm headline such as **"Gentle dentistry for the whole family."** set left over a soft mint-white hero band ({colors.surface}), with a short subtitle and two buttons: the **mint teal primary CTA** ({colors.primary}) "Book an appointment" and an outlined "Call the clinic" secondary. To the right, a floating **booking card** ({shadows.float}) with a treatment select, preferred date, and a "Check availability" button breaks out of the band with a soft teal-tinted shadow.

Below the hero, the page cycles through a sequence of calm sections: a treatments grid of pastel **icon tiles** (mint, pink, sky, sand, lavender, cream) each with a rounded 48px icon, a title, and a one-line description; a "Your first visit" strip of numbered step cards; a team section of warm portrait cards with credentials placeholders; an insurance and pricing section with accepted-insurer placeholders, a 3-tier price card row (check-up, hygiene, whitening — placeholder names) and a dense price table; a pink **new-patient highlight card** ({colors.card-tint-pink-bold}); an FAQ accordion; and a deep-teal closing CTA band with opening hours beside it. An **emergency strip** sits above the navigation on every page with the emergency number.

The system pairs **Quicksand** (rounded geometric display at 600–700) with **Nunito** (rounded humanist body at 400–600) — both soft-terminal faces, so headings and body share the same friendly character. Buttons are `{rounded.md}` (14px) rounded rectangles — NOT pills — and cards use `{rounded.lg}` (16px) consistently. Shadows are soft and tinted toward the ink or the teal, never gray-black.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Soft mint-white hero band ({colors.surface}) with a floating booking card — light, open, nothing clinical-cold
- **Mint teal primary** ({colors.primary}) for the booking CTA, carried with dark green-black text ({colors.on-primary}) for contrast
- **Deeper teal** ({colors.primary-deep}) for links and text on white — the same hue, tuned to hold 4.5:1
- Soft pink accent ({colors.accent-pink}) for gentle highlights: new-patient offers, reminder badges, the highlight card
- Pastel treatment tiles (mint, pink, sky, sand, lavender, cream) with rounded icon tiles
- Quicksand headings + Nunito body — rounded, approachable, legible for older patients
- 14px buttons, 16px cards, 20px booking card — generous but not bubbly
- Emergency red ({colors.emergency}) is the only hard colour, reserved for the emergency strip, badge, and button
- Left-aligned hero with a booking card beside it; mobile pins a booking bar to the bottom of the screen

## Colors

> Template pages: home, /treatments, /team, /book, /new-patients, /insurance-pricing, /contact (hours + location), /emergency, /faq.

### Brand & Primary
- **Mint Teal** ({colors.primary}): The booking signal — the "Book an appointment" button, the featured price card border, the active calendar day. Used only as a **large fill with dark text** ({colors.on-primary}); never as text on white.
- **Teal Pressed** ({colors.primary-pressed}): Pressed state of the primary button (#0B5F58). Because this fill is dark, the label switches to white ({colors.on-primary-pressed}), which holds 7.6:1.
- **Teal Deep** ({colors.primary-deep}): Text-weight teal (#0F766E) — inline links, outlined button labels, icon colour on tiles, focus borders. Holds 5.5:1 on white.
- **Teal Tint** ({colors.primary-tint}): Pale mint (#D5F5EF) — selected states, icon tile backgrounds, the "included" cell in the price table.
- **Brand Deep** ({colors.brand-deep}): Deep green-teal (#0B3F3A) — the closing CTA band, active pill tabs, the "deep" button.
- **Brand Deep Shade** ({colors.brand-deep-shade}): Shaded variant for footer accents inside the deep band.
- **Brand Deep Mid** ({colors.brand-deep-mid}): Lifted panel inside the deep band.
- **Link** ({colors.link}): Inline text link — the same deep teal as {colors.primary-deep}, kept as its own key so link colour can be tuned independently.
- **Link Pressed** ({colors.link-pressed}): Pressed-state link.

**Contrast rule for teal:** {colors.primary} (#14B8A6) is a light colour — white text on it reaches only 2.5:1, so white is never placed on it. Large fills use {colors.on-primary} (#052E2B, 5.9:1). Any teal that must carry text on white uses {colors.primary-deep} (#0F766E, 5.5:1). Pressed fills use {colors.primary-pressed} (#0B5F58) with white text (7.6:1).

### Font Family
**Quicksand** (display): Rounded geometric sans with soft terminals; loaded at 600 and 700. Fallbacks: 'Trebuchet MS', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif. Carries hero-display through heading-5.

**Nunito** (body): Rounded humanist sans designed for long reading; loaded at 400 and 600. Fallbacks: 'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif. Carries subtitle, body, captions, badges, and buttons. There is no monospace role on this site.

Quicksand ships 300–700, so no weight above 700 is used; the hero sits at 700 rather than a heavier cut. Nunito's 500 is not loaded — every "medium" role (button-md, body-md-medium, micro) uses 600 instead.

### Principles
- Relaxed hero leading (1.08) on 72px display — Quicksand's round bowls need a little air
- Mild negative letter-spacing on display sizes (-1.5px to -0.4px); none below 26px
- Generous body leading (1.60) so patient information and consent text read easily
- 700 weight for section headlines, 600 for card titles and buttons; 400 body
- Body never drops below 16px on patient-facing content; 14px is for tables and captions only

## Layout

### Spacing System
- **Base unit**: 4px (8px primary increment)
- **Tokens**: `{spacing.xxs}` (4px) through `{spacing.hero}` (112px)
- **Section rhythm**: Marketing pages use `{spacing.section-lg}` (96px); the pricing and new-patient pages tighten to `{spacing.section}` (64px)

### Grid & Container
- 1200px max-width with 32px gutters — slightly narrower than a product site so line lengths stay comfortable
- Treatments: 3-up tile grid at desktop (6 tiles), 2-up tablet, 1-up mobile
- Team: 3-up or 4-up portrait cards
- First visit: 4 numbered step cards in a row with a connecting dotted line
- Pricing: 3-tier price card row with a price table below; insurer placeholders in a 6-up logo row
- Homepage: left-aligned hero with the booking card beside it (7/5 split)

### Whitespace Philosophy
Calm surfaces use generous breathing room between sections. The booking card gets a soft teal-tinted float shadow so it reads as the one thing to do. Nothing crowds the emergency strip — it is one line, always.

## Elevation & Depth

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (flat) | No shadow; `{colors.hairline}` border | Price rows, FAQ items, patient-info cards |
| 1 (subtle) | `{shadows.soft}` — `0 1px 2px rgba(22, 48, 46, 0.04)` | Hover-lifted tiles |
| 2 (card) | `{shadows.card}` — `0 6px 20px rgba(22, 48, 46, 0.08)` | Treatment cards, team cards |

### Decorative Depth
- The hero booking card uses the teal-tinted float shadow (Level 3) — a gentle glow rather than a hard drop
- Pastel treatment tiles carry their own visual weight via tint backgrounds; they take no shadow
- Soft rounded blob shapes in {colors.card-tint-mint} and {colors.card-tint-pink} may sit behind the hero portrait at 40% opacity — organic, never geometric confetti

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `{colors.primary}` mint as the dominant booking CTA — with dark `{colors.on-primary}` text, always
- Use `{colors.primary-deep}` for any teal that carries text on white (links, outlined labels, icons)
- Pair the soft hero band with the floating booking card and the "Accepting new patients" badge
- Use pastel treatment tints (mint, pink, sky, sand, lavender, cream) to differentiate treatment families
- Use `{colors.accent-pink}` sparingly for the new-patient highlight and reminder badges
- Keep the emergency strip on every page, one line, with a tap-to-call number
- Apply `{rounded.md}` (14px) to buttons and `{rounded.lg}` (16px) to all card families
- Keep Quicksand for headings and Nunito for everything else
- Label every price "from" and every credential as supplied by the clinic

### Don't
- Don't put white text on `{colors.primary}` mint — it fails contrast
- Don't use mint for body text or large section backgrounds; use `{colors.surface}` for calm bands
- Don't use pill-shaped buttons; the geometry is rounded-rectangle, not bubble
- Don't mix `{colors.link}` with `{colors.primary}` fills — links are deep teal text, CTAs are mint fills
- Don't apply heavy gray shadows; shadows are soft and ink- or teal-tinted
- Don't use red anywhere except emergency and validation
- Don't invent reviews, before/after photos, credentials, insurer names, or prices — use labelled placeholders
- Don't use clinical stock imagery of instruments or close-up mouths; show people and the space

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints
| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile (small) | < 480px | Single column. Hero 34px. Price cards 1-up. Booking bar pinned. |
| Mobile (large) | 480 – 767px | Treatment tiles 2-up. Hero 42px. Booking bar pinned. |
| Tablet | 768 – 1023px | 2-column grids. Hero 52px. Booking card moves below hero text. |
| Desktop | 1024 – 1279px | 3-tier price row. Hero 64px. |
| Wide Desktop | ≥ 1280px | Full 72px hero presentation with booking card beside. |

### Touch Targets
- Buttons render at 44–48px effective height
- Form inputs render at 48px height
- Pill tabs ~36px → 44px on mobile
- The emergency number and the phone link are always ≥44px tap targets

### Collapsing Strategy
- **Emergency strip** stays full-width; shortens to "Emergency? Call [number]" at < 480px
- **Top nav** below 1024px collapses to hamburger; the "Book" button stays visible
- **Hero band**: booking card moves below text/buttons on tablet and becomes the pinned `mobile-booking-bar` on mobile
- **Price cards**: 3-column → 2-column tablet → 1-column mobile; price table scrolls horizontally with the first column sticky
- **Treatment tiles**: 3-up desktop → 2-up tablet → 1-up mobile
- **First-visit steps**: row of 4 → vertical list with the dotted connector on the left
- **Hero typography**: 72px → 52px → 42px → 34px
- **Footer**: 4-column desktop → 2-column tablet → accordion mobile

### Image Behavior
- Hero portrait keeps its 4:5 frame and moves above the headline on mobile
- Team portraits keep 3:4; grid collapses 4 → 2 → 1
- Insurer logo row: wordmarks at consistent 32–40px height, 6-up → 3-up

1. Focus on ONE component at a time
2. Reference component names and tokens directly
3. Run `npx @google/design.md lint DESIGN.md` after edits
4. Add new variants as separate `components:` entries
5. Default to `{typography.body-md}` for body at 16px
6. Keep `{colors.primary}` (mint) as the booking CTA with dark text; keep `{colors.primary-deep}` for links and icons
7. Use `{rounded.md}` for buttons, `{rounded.lg}` for cards, `{rounded.full}` for pill tabs/badges only
