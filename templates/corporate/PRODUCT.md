# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js route `/corporate` in the root app (its own components, tokens from this folder's DESIGN.md)

## Users

(inferred)

- **Prospective B2B customers** — operations, procurement or line-of-business managers evaluating a services or solutions provider. They want to confirm capability, scale, industries served, locations and credibility before they contact sales or issue an RFP. Desktop-heavy, often arriving mid-research with a shortlist.
- **Partners and suppliers** — checking what the company does, who leads it and how to engage.
- **Job candidates** — reading about culture, leadership and open roles; frequently on mobile.
- **Press and investors** — looking for news, leadership bios, financial or annual-report material and a media / IR contact.

## Product Purpose

(inferred) The site is the company's primary credibility and lead-generation surface. It explains what the company does and for whom, proves scale and seriousness (locations, leadership, certifications, news), and routes visitors to the right conversation. Success looks like qualified "contact sales" conversations and RFP requests, strong applicant flow to careers, and press and investors finding what they need without calling.

## Positioning

Placeholder, to be written by the company: "[Company] is the [sector] partner that [the claim a generic competitor could not truthfully copy — e.g. a proprietary method, a specific accreditation, a geographic footprint, or an outcome guarantee]." The template ships with this line marked as a placeholder.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive via search ("[service] provider [region]"), referrals and tenders, professional social networks, press coverage, partner links and investor-relations links.
- Desktop dominates for buyers and investors; mobile for candidates and news readers. The site must be fast and fully usable on both.
- Use is researched and multi-visit; a buyer may read services, industries, leadership and news across several sessions before contacting sales.
- Offline touchpoints the site must support: phone to regional offices, email to sales and media contacts, downloadable company overview or capability statement, and physical office locations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Required pages / sections**: home, services / solutions (overview + detail per service), industries, about (history, values), leadership (executive and board cards), news (press releases, insights), careers (culture + open roles), locations (offices with addresses), investors (optional: reports, governance), contact (contact-sales form + regional contacts), legal (privacy, cookies, terms, accessibility statement, modern-slavery or ESG statements as applicable).
- **Primary CTA**: "Contact sales" (form). **Secondary**: "See careers", "Download company overview", "Request a proposal", "Media enquiries".
- **Contact-sales form fields** (undecided, suggested): name, work email, company, role, company size, country, topic / service of interest, message; consent checkbox.
- **Must-have information**: registered office and company number placeholders, regional office addresses and phones, media and investor contacts, certifications and memberships placeholders, copyright and policies.
- **Integrations typically needed**: CRM or marketing-automation form capture, applicant tracking system for roles, newsroom / CMS, analytics with consent management, optional investor-relations feed.
- **Legal / compliance**: privacy and cookie consent, regulatory statements per jurisdiction, accessibility statement, financial-disclosure rules if listed (placeholder).
- **Explicitly undecided**: CMS choice, whether investor pages exist, number of services and industries, gated vs. open downloads, languages.

## Brand Commitments

- Business name, logo, imagery, statistics and voice are placeholders to be supplied by the company.
- Inferred voice for the industry: confident, structured, specific; sentence-case headlines; claims backed by named evidence; no jargon without definition; consistent terminology for services across pages.

## Evidence on Hand

No real statistics, client logos, case studies, testimonials, leadership names or photos, press releases, certifications, financial figures or office addresses exist in this template, and none may be fabricated. The owner must supply: service descriptions, industry lists, leadership bios and portraits, permission-cleared client and partner logos, audited statistics, news items, office details, certifications, open roles, and legal texts.

## Product Principles

1. **Prove, don't proclaim.** Every claim of scale or quality is backed by a supplied fact; placeholders stay labelled until then.
2. **Route every visitor.** Buyers, candidates, press and investors each reach their destination within two clicks from the home page.
3. **Consistency is credibility.** Services, industries and leadership are described with the same structure and terminology everywhere.
4. **Keep it current.** News, leadership and locations must be easy to update; stale pages damage trust faster than missing ones.
5. **Global by default.** Addresses, phone formats, dates and legal statements are written to work across regions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text on white meets 4.5:1; amber is used only on navy with large figures or ink-labelled buttons.
- Mega-menus, tabs and accordions are keyboard-operable with visible focus and correct ARIA roles.
- Leadership and news lists are navigable with screen readers (headings, lists, dates in text).
- Forms have visible labels, error messages tied to fields, and no time limits.
- Multilingual readiness: layout tolerates 30 % longer strings; language switcher placement reserved in the nav.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; charts and diagrams have text equivalents.
