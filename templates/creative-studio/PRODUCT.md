# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js route `/creative-studio` in the root app (its own components, tokens from this folder's DESIGN.md)

## Users
- Founders, marketing leads and product teams shortlisting a design studio for brand identity, web, content or motion work.
- Candidates looking at the team and culture.

## Product Purpose
Turn interest into a project conversation: show the work, the people, the prices and the process, then make "Get in touch" one tap away.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections: hero, stats, portfolio, services, why us, testimonials, team, pricing, FAQ, news, contact, footer.
- Primary CTA: "Get in touch" form (name, email, project). Secondary: "Who we are", "See our work", "Subscribe now" per plan.
- No figures, prices, client names or quotes may be invented; every slot ships as a labelled placeholder.

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: short, confident, plain. Sentence-case headlines.
- The lime is the only accent; it never carries white text.

## Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA; pills meet 44px; FAQ and tabs keyboard-operable; reduced motion honoured.
