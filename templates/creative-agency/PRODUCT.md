# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js route `/creative-agency` in the root app (its own components, tokens from this folder's DESIGN.md)

## Users
(inferred)

- **Brand and marketing heads evaluating a studio.** A brand director or CMO with a rebrand, launch or campaign to commission. They arrive via referral or a piece of work they admired, usually on desktop, and are judging taste first: is the work excellent, is it varied, is it relevant to our sector, and does the studio think the way we do. Their job on the site is to shortlist and start a conversation.
- **Founders looking for a launch partner.** Often mobile, often late at night, less process-aware; they want to see work that feels like their ambition, understand what the studio can do end to end, and reach a person quickly.
- **Press and talent.** Journalists looking for the studio's story, people and awards; designers, strategists and producers checking the work, the culture and open roles.

## Product Purpose
(inferred)

The site exists to make the quality of the work undeniable and to turn that admiration into new project conversations — and, secondarily, into press coverage and job applications. Success looks like: "Start a project" enquiries from brands and founders with real budgets, case-page depth of engagement (time, scroll, video plays), press contact, and inbound applications. The site is also the studio's portfolio of record, so case pages must hold up as individual destinations.

## Positioning
Placeholder, to be written by the owner: the thing a generic studio site could not truthfully say — a craft discipline the studio is known for, a sector it has shaped, a method, a scale of ambition, or a refusal (what it will not make). Until supplied, the template uses neutral copy and no comparative claims.

## Operating Context
- **Arrival:** referrals from clients and peers; a specific case shared on social or in a pitch; design-award and press listings; search for the studio name; talent arriving from job boards and the team's own social presence.
- **Devices and moments:** evaluative and unhurried on desktop (brand heads reviewing with a team), quick and mobile from social links (founders, talent, press). Both must get to the work in one tap.
- **Offline touchpoints the site must support:** a named contact or new-business email, a phone number, a studio address and visiting information, and a press kit (logo, boilerplate, founder bios, selected images) available on request or download.
- **Performance context:** the site is image- and video-heavy by nature; its reputation depends on these loading fast and looking perfect on retina and mobile. Media performance is a brand issue, not just a technical one.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Required pages/sections:** home (hero, client marquee, selected work, manifesto, services, contact); work index (filterable by discipline and sector) and individual case pages (hero media, client/sector/services/year, narrative, image and video bands, outcome, next case); studio (manifesto, people, culture, awards placeholder, press contact); services/capabilities (numbered list with scope); contact (full-screen block with form and direct details); careers (optional); legal (privacy, cookies).
- **Primary CTAs:** "Start a project" (nav, hero, contact block). **Secondary:** "See work", "Next case", "Press enquiries", "Join us".
- **Forms/flows:** project enquiry (name, company, email, what you are making, timeline, budget range, how you found us), press enquiry, application link. Calendar booking is **undecided**.
- **Must-have information:** the work itself with accurate credits, services and capabilities, people and roles, studio location and contact, press kit availability, awards (only real ones), accessibility statement.
- **Integrations typically needed:** image/video CDN with responsive formats, video hosting, form handling/CRM, analytics, applicant tracking (optional).
- **Legal/compliance:** every piece of work shown requires client permission and correct credits (collaborators, photographers, typefaces where licensed); client logos in the marquee require permission; awards must be real and dated; image rights and model releases for photography; privacy notice for form data; cookie consent where required; respect any client NDAs and embargoes.
- **Explicitly undecided:** number of cases at launch, video versus still-led case templates, careers section, calendar booking, awards section until real awards exist.

## Brand Commitments
The studio's name, logo, wordmark and voice are placeholders to be supplied by the owner. Inferred voice guidance for the industry: confident, spare, specific; let the work speak and keep copy short; name the idea behind each case in one sentence; avoid agency clichés ("we craft", "passionate"); be generous in crediting collaborators; the manifesto states beliefs, not services.

## Evidence on Hand
No real work, case studies, client names or logos, awards, press, team members, photographs, video or results exist in this template, and none may be fabricated. The marquee ships with "CLIENT ONE · CLIENT TWO" placeholders, the work grid with placeholder tiles, and the awards strip with "AWARD PLACEHOLDER" labels. The owner must supply: case studies with client permission, credits and media in the required aspect ratios and formats; client logos with permission; team names, roles and portraits; the manifesto; real awards and press; the press kit; studio address and contact details.

## Product Principles
1. **The work is the argument.** Every path leads to the work within one tap; copy, colour and type serve it and never compete with it.
2. **Performance is craft.** Images and video must load fast and look flawless; a slow or blurry case page undermines the studio's claim to excellence.
3. **Credit everything.** Collaborators, photographers and clients are named accurately; permissions are secured before anything is published.
4. **Taste over volume.** Fewer, stronger cases beat a long index; the work grid is curated, not exhaustive.
5. **A point of view, plainly stated.** The manifesto and services say what the studio believes and does; vagueness reads as insecurity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.2 AA target; white canvas with near-black text; white text on cobalt ≥ 4.5:1; near-black text on orange (never white on orange).
- Oversized headlines must remain readable when zoomed to 200% and reflow without horizontal scrolling; use real text, never images of type.
- Every image carries meaningful alt text describing the work; videos have poster images, captions or transcripts where there is speech, and never autoplay with sound.
- Work-card hover reveals are also triggered by keyboard focus and are replaced by always-visible titles on touch devices.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the marquee, reveals and scroll animations; provide a static alternative.
- Visible focus states on the contact form; error messages in text, not colour alone.
- Press kit and careers documents provided in accessible formats; consider multilingual case summaries where the studio works internationally.
