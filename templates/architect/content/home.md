### 6. How a project runs
- Eyebrow: How a project runs
- Heading: Four stages, explained plainly
- Stage 1 — Brief: We listen, measure the site, and agree what the building needs to do and roughly what it can cost. You leave with a written brief and a fee proposal.
- Stage 2 — Design: Sketches first, then drawings and a model. We present options, not a single answer, and refine the one you choose until the plans are fixed.
- Stage 3 — Permission: We prepare and submit the planning and building applications, and handle the questions that come back.
- Stage 4 — Construction: We produce the drawings builders price from, help you choose a contractor, and visit the site regularly until the keys are handed over.
- Link: The full process
